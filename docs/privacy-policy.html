Privacy Policy — Onda

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Package ID: io.github.mcello23.onda

Summary

Onda is a free, open-source radio and podcast player for Android and Android Auto. Onda does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no advertisements, no analytics SDKs, and no trackers.

Data Stored Locally

Favorites, recently played stations, song history, podcast subscriptions, offline episode downloads, and settings stay strictly on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Third-Party Network Connections

When you browse or play media, your device connects directly to:

  • Radio Browser API (*.api.radio-browser.info) for station catalog search and anonymous click counting.
  • Apple iTunes Search API (itunes.apple.com) for podcast search and charts.
  • Google Favicon Service (www.google.com/s2/favicons) as a fallback for missing station logos.
  • Third-party broadcasters and podcast hosts for audio streams, RSS feeds, and artwork.

Those third parties receive standard HTTP/HTTPS network metadata (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies. Onda operates no developer backend server.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: github.com/mcello23/onda/issues

Política de Privacidade — Onda (PT-BR)

O Onda é um reprodutor gratuito e de código aberto de rádios e podcasts para Android e Android Auto. O Onda não coleta, armazena, vende ou compartilha dados pessoais. Não há contas, anúncios, analytics ou rastreamento. Todos os seus favoritos, podcasts e ajustes ficam salvos apenas no seu aparelho.

Ao buscar ou ouvir conteúdo, o app conecta-se diretamente ao Radio Browser, à iTunes Search API, ao serviço de favicons do Google (fallback de logotipos) e aos servidores das próprias emissoras e podcasts. Dúvidas: github.com/mcello23/onda/issues.
